01 / OVERVIEW

Understanding the Market

CareSync is a healthcare startup exploring technology-driven solutions to improve the healthcare experience.

With more than 700 prospective customers registered on its waitlist, the startup demonstrated early market interest. My role focused on supporting customer discovery and business strategy by engaging directly with prospective customers and collaborating with company leadership.

02 / OBJECTIVE

From Interest to Customer Insight

A growing waitlist provides an important indication of market interest. However, understanding why customers are interested and what they expect from a product is essential to building a sustainable business.

KEY OBJECTIVE

Understand prospective customer needs and translate their feedback into actionable business insights.
