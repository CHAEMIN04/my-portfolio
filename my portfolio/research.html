01 / RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Bridging Statistical Research and Practical Applications

My undergraduate research focuses on developing accessible computational tools for advanced statistical analysis, particularly the study of relationships between mixed-type data.

Working under the supervision of Dr. Zhaohu (Jonathan) Fan, I contribute to a funded research initiative that combines statistical methodology, software development, and applied business analytics.

Our work seeks to make sophisticated statistical methods more accessible to researchers and practitioners through reusable Python implementations and reproducible analytical workflows.

RESEARCH OBJECTIVE

Transform advanced statistical methodologies into accessible, reproducible tools that support data-driven research and decision-making.

02 / RESEARCH PROBLEM

Understanding Relationships in Complex Data

Real-world datasets frequently contain a combination of continuous, categorical, ordinal, and binary variables. Traditional correlation methods can be limited when analyzing relationships across these different data types, especially when additional variables influence the observed associations.

Our research investigates surrogate partial association methods to better understand relationships between mixed-type variables while accounting for relevant covariates.

01

Mixed-Type Data

Developing analytical approaches that accommodate different variable types within a unified statistical framework.

02

Partial Association

Investigating relationships between variables after accounting for the influence of relevant covariates.

03

Statistical Inference

Evaluating uncertainty and the reliability of estimated associations through statistical inference.

04

Reproducibility

Making research methods accessible through reusable software, documentation, and consistent workflows.

04 / TECHNICAL DEVELOPMENT

Building Reusable Research Software

A central component of our research is the development of a Python implementation of surrogate partial association methodology. The toolkit supports researchers working with mixed-type datasets and provides a foundation for reproducible statistical analysis.
